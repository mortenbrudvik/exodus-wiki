Design exploration · 25 July 2026

Five visual directions

Each mockup shows the same two real pages — the Finn Jalgori-Tobu article and the Characters hub — so they can be compared directly. Use the Article / Hub switch in the black bar at the top of each.

What the current design gets wrong

  • Nothing on the page says which book this is. The chrome would suit a corporate intranet equally well.
  • Prose reads as blue noise — on the Finn article roughly one word in six is a link, all in saturated #0b57d0.
  • No dark mode, on a site about a novel most people read at night.
  • 59 illustrations exist. A reader sees each one exactly once, in a 240 px box, only after already finding the page. No hub shows a single face.
  • Long articles have no table of contents and no sense of position.
  • The 11-item sidebar is one flat list — "Chapters" sits at the same level as "All book wikis".
  • Category hubs are walls of bullets. Characters is 40 names in three columns of grey text.
  • A row-gap bug leaves ~55 px of dead space between every h1 and its lead paragraph.

The five

  1. 01

    Vector

    The wiki's only job is reading. Spend everything on measure, hierarchy and orientation; wear no costume at all.

    Layout
    Grouped sidebar · 68ch serif column · right rail carrying a sticky section index above the infobox
    Signature
    The section index that tracks where you are in the article
    Costs
    It is the least distinctive of the five. Nothing about it says Exodus.
    Open Vector →
  2. 02

    Armorial

    The spine of the novel is dynastic — houses, Dominions, queens, consorts, mindline succession. Build the wiki like a peerage, and let every subject wear its allegiance.

    Layout
    Allegiance spine in the house colour · tinted title plate with a geometric sigil · the infobox unboxed into a ruled armorial entry
    Signature
    The house rail — succession drawn as a diagram instead of listed as a field
    Costs
    Needs a colour and a sigil assigned per house, which is new data to maintain. Works beautifully for Celestials and Uranics, awkwardly for Travelers and technology pages.
    Open Armorial →
  3. 03

    Starfield

    Space opera, read at night, with 59 dark illustrations going unused. Make dark the default rather than an afterthought, and let the pictures carry the page.

    Layout
    Dossier panel with a full-bleed portrait · monospace for every datum · hub as a portrait card grid
    Signature
    The connections strip — "who moved him", as faces rather than a See-also list
    Costs
    Long prose on dark is harder for some readers, so the light theme has to be equally good, not a fallback. Relationship labels are new data per character.
    Open Starfield →
  4. 04

    Plate

    Hamilton is an engineer's writer, and the pages are so densely cross-linked the prose disappears. Treat the wiki as a technical manual and pull the references out of the sentence into the margin.

    Layout
    Fixed 62ch measure with a permanent apparatus column · hub as a dense sortable index table
    Signature
    Margin references — the body text carries superscripts, and each one is resolved with a gloss in the margin
    Costs
    The biggest bet here, and building it exposed the real limit: margin notes only stay beside their sentence if their density matches the prose. At 19 notes the margin ran a screen ahead of the text; trimmed to 11 it holds. So this direction caps how much can be cross-referenced — which is either the whole point or a serious constraint, depending on your view. Every reference also needs a hand-written one-line gloss, and on mobile the margin collapses inline.
    Open Plate →
  5. 05

    Atlas

    The illustrations are the most striking thing the wiki owns and they are buried. Lead with them: the picture is the way in.

    Layout
    Hero band with the subject image · key-facts strip instead of a side infobox · narrow 40rem reading column · hub as large tiles behind an A–Z jump bar
    Signature
    The hero band, and the film strip of people that closes the article
    Costs
    The first build cropped a 3:4 portrait into a wide band and decapitated it, so the band now shows the picture whole against a blurred copy of itself — no crop decision per subject, but it means the hero is atmosphere rather than a full-bleed image. Pages without an illustration still need a designed fallback, and the first line of prose sits below the fold.

Constraints every direction respects

  • No build step, no dependencies. All five are plain CSS with system font stacks — nothing is fetched from a CDN, and every page still works over file://.
  • The 25 generated pages. Whichever direction wins, the markup change has to be mirrored into gen-celestials.mjs and gen-dominions.mjs or the next run reverts it.
  • Page chrome invariants. Skip link, data-root, favicon, the title suffix and aria-current are all checked by check-wiki.mjs; the mockups keep them in mind but do not implement the full chrome.
  • Illustration provenance stays reader-facing on sources.html, never in article prose. Directions 3 and 5 lean hard on the images, so the "inferred, not canonical" caption has to travel with them.

These are mockups, not implementations. They live in docs/, which pages.yml deletes before publishing, so nothing here ships to the live site.
