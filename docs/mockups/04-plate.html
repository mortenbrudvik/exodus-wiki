Finn Jalgori-Tobu is a ~25-year-old Uranic1 aristocrat from Gondiar and the adventure-thread protagonist of Exodus: The Archimedes Engine. Restless under Crown Dominion client-rule, he survives a mercenary dump over Anoosha, bonds with the late arkship Diligent2, and becomes a driving force behind seizing and using an Archimedes Engine3 on Dolod — for human liberation, and under Gyvoy Enfoe’s manipulation.

§ 1

Background

4CelestialsThe ruling non-human clades, and the Dominions they hold
5TravelersFree spacefarers; what Finn wants to be instead of a minister

Finn is the second son of the Jalgori-Tobu family, client rulers who administer human society on Gondiar for the Celestials4 of the Crown Dominion. As Minsterialis of Hafnir, he holds land and status, but he longs to travel space as a Traveler5 rather than settle into the family’s proxy-government role. Earth is only a history footnote to him — he is born into Celestial client order, not the exodus memory that still shapes Diligent humans.

6LivestoneCelestial material, shaped directly through a neural interface

Like other Uranics, he carries neural interfaces that let him work Celestial materials such as livestone6. His twin sister Otylia, Minsterialis of Serki, is a more institutional counterweight: she manages family and ministerial politics while Finn drifts toward adventure and, later, open resistance. Older siblings include the heir Zelinda Maryann Richelle and brother Everett Callan Mathias.

§ 2

Anoosha and the Diligent alliance

7Eleanor AponiFinds Finn on Anoosha; runs the salvage expeditions
8Josias AponiHer grandfather; owner of the ship, and its politics

The novel opens with Finn bound and thrown from a plane over the Camurdy Mountains on Anoosha after a failed heist involving the mercenary Liliana. Injured, he shapes livestone into shelter until he is found by Eleanor (Ellie) Aponi7 and her grandfather Josias8, who have crash-landed from the newly arrived arkship.

9ZPZ generatorWhat makes the arkship a true deep-space vessel

Finn and Josias strike a bargain: land at Hafnir for the Diligent’s settlers in exchange for Finn’s path off-world. Back on Gondiar the family accepts the settlement deal as economically useful; Finn and Ellie later travel to recover the arkship and pursue a ZPZ generator9.

§ 3

ZPZ, the Engine, and the endgame

10Human liberationThe cause Gyvoy invokes to get the Engine fired

Finn’s long arc takes him through salvage at Terrik Papuan, clashes with Traveler interests including captain Andino, a dangerous extraction on Kajval, and the hunt for Engine operating software at Kingsnest. Gyvoy Enfoe steers him toward using an Engine on Dolod to keep the gas giant from settling usefully into the Kelowan system — framed as a blow for human liberation10.

11BoksrockThe endgame target of the redirected momentum transfer

On Dolod, Gyvoy’s influence and Finn’s grief over Crown violence push the crisis to a head. Finn issues Engine commands that redirect momentum transfers toward Boksrock11 rather than a simple Kelowan-denial plan; later he engineers a second transfer that accelerates the Diligent via an anchor ship and its ZPZ field, allowing the arkship to flee toward Capo Frois. Ellie recovers suppressed memories of Gyvoy’s non-consensual interface with Finn’s induction pad, exposing the manipulation behind the operation.
