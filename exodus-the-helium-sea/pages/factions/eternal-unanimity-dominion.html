The Eternal Unanimity Dominion is a Celestial polity of the Centauri Cluster, associated with the cluster’s centre rather than with the Crown Dominion theatre that dominates Exodus: The Archimedes Engine. On the wider Cluster map it stands as one of the major independent dominions; only that placement and its near neighbours are fixed on this page so far.

Place on the Cluster map

The Eternal Unanimity Dominion holds the centre of the Centauri Cluster. That central position is the main fixed fact: other named polities are measured relative to it. The Quichi Dominion occupies several star systems close to Unanimity territory.

Celestial Dominions as a class are high-caste powers that send archons as operatives and control swaths of Cluster space. Unanimity sits among them on the same map as the first novel’s better-staged polities — Crown, Heresy, Talloch-Te, Mara Yama, and others — without yet receiving the same court-level coverage here.
