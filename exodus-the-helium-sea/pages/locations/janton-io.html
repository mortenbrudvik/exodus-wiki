Janton-Io is a named world in The Helium Sea: a LoOx (low-oxygen) environment whose resident Celestials are known for a deliberate, extreme low-energy way of life rather than for fleets, courts, or megastructures. On the Cluster map of specialized Celestial projects it stands as a lifestyle pole opposite industrious or political dominions.

LoOx world

Janton-Io is associated with a low-oxygen environment. That setting is part of what makes its Celestials’ project distinctive: not only culture but habitat conditions push a different relationship to energy, pace, and engagement with other powers.

Lifestyle specialization

Where the Eternal Unanimity Dominion is measured by central Cluster power, and the Gomatu by a Dyson-sphere megastructure, the Celestials of Janton-Io are characterized by what they primarily do with existence: an extreme low-energy, non-belligerent lifestyle — slow, energy-conserving living on a LoOx world rather than fleets or court pageantry. That is specialization as much as architecture is for other Celestials.

They are not framed as a conquest power. The point of naming them is contrast: Celestial civilizations diverge not only in body plan and tech but in chosen ends.
