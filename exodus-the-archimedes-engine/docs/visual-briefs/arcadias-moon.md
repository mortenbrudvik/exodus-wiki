## Physical / design cues (research)

- True hull (novel p. 241 / wiki): a **geodesic sphere of golden trusses** enclosing **eight ovoid elements** that slide and realign around the interior as flight status changes
- Mid-size Traveler deniable charter — not navy capital, not arkship-scale
- Silhouette is unmistakable enough that concealing it requires a full shell fuselage (*Infinite Totality*)
