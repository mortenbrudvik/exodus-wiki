Dagon is the offworld Celestial-linked operator who enters Santa Rosa gang networks as Gyvoy Enfoe’s contact, operates the Gyvoy imposture that steers the Engine plot, and is revealed as Thyra’s uncle.

Crime-network entry point

Terence’s early case: Younes must introduce Dagon to Gyvoy; Younes dies; Colvin immolates. Zelinda conveys the Governor’s order not to pursue Dagon.

Imposture and Dolod

Late investigation finds Gyvoy’s body as a discarded shell. Ellie recovers forced induction-pad memories. Endgame recaps: Dagon is Thyra’s uncle, binding Engine manipulation to Bekket’s dynasty war.
