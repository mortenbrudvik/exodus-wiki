Avone (minor)

General Avone-Valerio commands the hardline navy fleet that locks down Gondiar after Makaio’s murder — ghost units, awakened animals, navy HQ in Jalgori-Tobu spaces, and the occupation that radicalizes the liberation crisis.

Gondiar occupation

Manipulated by Uulana-Shoigu, Empress Carolien-Amaia dispatches Avone-Valerio. Josias’s strike broadcast, airstrikes killing the Marchioness of Santa Rosa, and the Jalgori-Tobu flight from Zetian Palace all occur under this occupation.
