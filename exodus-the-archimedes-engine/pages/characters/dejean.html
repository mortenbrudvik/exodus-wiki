Dejean

Role

Affiliation
Diligent crew; Diligent humans
Works with

Dejean is captain of the arkship Diligent, responsible for shipboard command while politics, salvage, and Engine ops pull the hull into Traveler and Dominion crossfire. Where Josias voices the ship as orator and Ellie leads field missions, Dejean keeps the vessel runnable through refits, relativistic campaigns, and the final flight from Dolod.
