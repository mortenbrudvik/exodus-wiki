Bekket (also called “Oneshot” in court context) is the father of Thyra through a liaison with Queen Helena-Chione around a Dominion coronation cycle. He later reveals aspects of Thyra’s heritage that reframe her ambitions, and after her mindline coup he is installed as master of the Wynid court while hardline archons reshape policy. Rival investigation into the Bekket network (e.g. Uulana-Lyon) meets slowball ambush and assassination in wider recaps.
