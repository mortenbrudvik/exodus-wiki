Characters

People of Exodus: The Archimedes Engine — Uranic aristocrats and rebels, arkship settlers, Celestial royalty and archons, Travelers, and those caught between Dominion order and human liberation. Full spoilers throughout.

Every character page is listed below. For Celestial naming conventions, the royal houses, and figures named only in passing, see the Celestials roster.

Arkship Diligent

  • Josias Aponi — owner of the Diligent; orator who turns settlement into liberation politics
  • Eleanor Aponi — his granddaughter; lieutenant who finds Finn and runs the salvage expeditions
  • Dejean — captain of the arkship; professional counterweight to Josias’s politics
  • Elsbeth McQuillan — hired armour commander on the Kajval dropship raid

Gondiar: the Jalgori-Tobus and the law

  • Finn Jalgori-Tobu — Uranic aristocrat; Minsterialis of Hafnir; the adventure thread’s protagonist
  • Otylia Jalgori-Tobu — Finn’s twin; Minsterialis of Serki; marries Josias
  • Zelinda Jalgori-Tobu — eldest sister and heir; the institutional face of Uranic rule
  • Terence Wilson-Fletcher — Santa Rosa detective; later carries the Makaio-Spirit
  • Neusch — Makaio-Faraji’s son; the archon mindline’s living continuation

Now and Forever Queens

  • Helena-Chione — Queen of Wynid; her mindline is usurped to become Helena-Thyra
  • Carolien-Amaia — Queen of Verak; holds the rotating imperial throne through the crisis
  • Luus — Queen of Bassa; the Empress who hands the throne to Carolien
  • Ramona-Ursule — Queen of Nizinsk; hosts Thyra’s politics trial
  • Inessa-Pierina — Queen of Cheluli; peer seat of the Imperial Accord
  • Zuberi-Dulcina — former Queen of Kelowan; the Accord was founded against her

The Wynid court

  • Thyra — congregant daughter who wins the trials and seizes Helena’s mindline
  • Clavissa — her half-sister; a trial survivor kept close and constrained
  • Bekket — Uixic count; Thyra’s father; Master of the Court after the coup
  • Lord Jolav — Helena’s consort and Clavissa’s father; a quiet opposition node
  • Lord Valdier — Helena’s father; Master of the Court in the coronation era
  • Siskala — Major of the Royal Tiger Guard; Helena’s “new life” daughter
  • Lord Stethos-Thierry — court datamaster; permanently interfaced to answer the queen’s queries
  • Lord Gahiji — Chief Archon to Helena-Chione; edged out after the coup
  • Malquilvo — court parent killed for accusing Thyra after the survival trial
  • Uulana — the Lyon investigation, and the Shoigu identity that replaces it

Archons and Celestial agents

  • Makaio — Wynid archon across two hosts; spycraft from the Dolod warning to Gondiar
  • Acelynn — Chief Archon to Carolien-Amaia of Verak
  • Asahi-Iryna — takes Wynid oversight after Makaio, then the pursuit of the Diligent
  • Olomo — Archon of the Heresy Dominion; Dolod science
  • Sahdiah — Talloch-Te archon who runs Travelers as deniable sensors
  • Avone-Valerio — general of the navy fleet that locks Gondiar down
  • Radwarno — Verak commander ordered to capture the fleeing arkship
  • Dagon — the Celestial operator behind the Gyvoy Enfoe imposture

Travelers, mercenaries and fixers

  • Gyvoy Enfoe — the financier identity that steers the Engine plot; a mask worn by Dagon
  • Andino — captain of Arcadia’s Moon
  • Marcellu — Traveler middleman carrying Sahdiah’s brief against the Diligent
  • Medusa — assassin who passes from Marcellu’s crew into Sahdiah’s service
  • Liliana — mercenary who opens the novel by dumping Finn over the Camurdy Mountains
  • Toše — her loud counterpart: the Gondiar bomb and the shot that kills Makaio
