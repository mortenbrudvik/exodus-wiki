Olomo is a Celestial archon active in the multi-system intelligence game around the novel’s mid-to-late plot. Timeline and investigative beats place Olomo among recipients of warning messages about Gyvoy Enfoe imposture (with peers such as Sahdiah and Asahi-Iryna) as vectors converge on Dolod. Through Terence’s Makaio-Spirit protocols and related archon channels, Olomo marks how high Celestial politics already tracks the “human” Engine plot.
