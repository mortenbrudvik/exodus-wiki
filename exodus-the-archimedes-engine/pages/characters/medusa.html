Medusa

Medusa

Assassin / operative; Marcellu’s right-hand → Sahdiah asset

Tasked by archon Sahdiah (Talloch-Te / nomad trader dominion); Traveler logistics
Ship contact
Andino / Arcadia’s Moon

Terence Wilson-Fletcher
Reputation
Ruthless assassin working for Celestial interests

Medusa is a ruthless field assassin who moves from Marcellu’s Traveler crew into archon Sahdiah’s service after her principal’s murder. On Gondiar she becomes a central case for Terence Wilson-Fletcher — contracting abductions, admitting Talloch-Te tasking, and later helping him pressure the archon board about Gyvoy while he still tries to keep her alive under navy lockdown.

From Marcellu’s right-hand to Sahdiah’s asset

After Liliana kills Marcellu with a cherenkov blade, Andino takes Medusa aboard the Arcadia’s Moon under summons from Sahdiah — a Talloch-Te archon of a nomad trader dominion. Sahdiah suspects Olomo of ordering Marcellu’s death and tasks Medusa to investigate. That assignment places a human-scale killer inside inter-archon blame games: Travelers as deniable sensors for Celestial Great Game moves.

Earlier, Marcellu and Andino have already flown to meet Sahdiah with a brief to prevent the Diligent from acquiring a ZPZ generator — a “random factor” Sahdiah wants out of Cluster politics. Medusa inherits that world of ghost fleets, salvage interference, and archon errands even as her personal loyalty chain breaks with Marcellu’s death.

Gondiar: Bopbe, Bersch, and Terence’s case

On Gondiar, Terence investigates the Bopbe kidnap: Varmor is contracted for it, Celestial-grade escape tech is involved. That investigation and related gang work point toward Medusa. Bersch confesses she contracted him to abduct supplier Zikar. Terence arrests her for the Bopbe attempt; she confirms she works for Sahdiah and that the snatch was an attempt to find Marcellu’s killer. She passes Sahdiah’s detail: the killer used a cherenkov blade and was a female associate of Marcellu’s — the clue that locks Liliana into Terence’s facial-match timeline with Gyvoy.

Medusa also reveals Toše’s contact with the captain of the Gansvoort, feeding the manhunt that eventually ties bombs, the Cybele’s Eagle, and Makaio-Faraji’s sniper death into one network. She is both suspect and informant — an asset who knows too much to free and too much to ignore.

Lockdown, fake death, and Sahdiah’s Dolod burn

When General Avone-Valerio’s navy locks Gondiar, Terence helps Medusa escape into a hospice. After Gyvoy’s body is found to be an impostor trail, he wakes her and fakes her death so she can message Sahdiah without open channels. Sahdiah receives that message, sees the Diligent vector toward Dolod, and burns for the gas giant — converging with Olomo, Thyra’s fleet politics, and the Engine crisis.

Even as the Makaio-Spirit hybrid pulls Terence into inter-archon protocols, he still counts Medusa among the people he tries to protect alongside Jimena and Vanilda. Her arc is the assassin who never becomes a POV hero yet carries the critical links, running from Marcellu to Sahdiah, from Sahdiah to Liliana’s blade, and from there to the Dolod board. Without her admissions, the detective thread cannot connect Traveler murder to Crown capital catastrophe.
