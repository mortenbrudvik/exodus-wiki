Medusa

Role
Operative / asset in archon–Traveler threads
Affiliation
Tasked by archon Sahdiah; contact with Andino / Travelers
Investigated by
Terence Wilson-Fletcher

Medusa is a secondary operative whose tasking from archon Sahdiah ties Traveler logistics to the wider ZPZ and liberation underworld. After Marcellu’s death and related Traveler intrigue she is taken aboard Andino’s Arcadia’s Moon; on Gondiar she becomes a case thread for Terence, who works her after she admits Sahdiah’s assignment. She remains among the people Terence still tries to protect as the Makaio-Spirit hybrid pulls him into inter-archon politics.
