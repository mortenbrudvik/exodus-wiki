Lord Jolav (Jolav-Dabny) is consort to Queen Helena-Chione and father of Clavissa. After Thyra’s mindline coup he becomes a quiet opposition node — comparing notes with sidelined archons and asking Clavissa to spy on Helena-Thyra’s early Kelowan fleet.

Consort and father

Jolav is Helena’s consort and Clavissa’s father, distinct from Bekket’s liaison that produces Thyra. He represents the court family line that loses when Bekket’s daughter seizes the mindline.

Opposition under Helena-Thyra

Clavissa confides distrust of Thyra to Jolav. When Thyra’s fleet leaves early for Kelowan, Jolav asks Clavissa to spy and points her to Neusch. He joins Gahiji and Makaio as residual elites map Uulana-Shoigu’s infiltration.
