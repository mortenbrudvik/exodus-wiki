Lord Valdier

Lord Valdier

Full style
Lord Valdier-Mímir
Role
Father of Helena-Chione; Master of the Court
Affiliation
Wynid royal household

Lord Valdier (Valdier-Mímir) is father of Queen Helena-Chione and Master of the Court in coronation-era scenes — the older generation of Wynid protocol who introduces guests such as Count Bekket.

Master of the Court

Valdier is Helena’s father and Master of the Court. He manages ballroom introductions as queens circulate with entourages. He is house etiquette continuity, distinct from Bekket’s later hardline master-of-court role under Helena-Thyra.
