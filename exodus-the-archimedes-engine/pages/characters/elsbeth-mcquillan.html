Elsbeth McQuillan

Role
Drive boss / specialist on Kajval extraction
Affiliation
Diligent campaign allies; field ops with Finn & Ellie
Notable op
Kajval dropship theft; path toward Kingsnest Engine software

Elsbeth McQuillan appears in expedition recaps as the drive boss of a dangerous Kajval extraction — a dropship theft that helps Finn, Ellie, and Diligent allies press toward Kingsnest and Archimedes Engine operating software. She is a specialist-side name in the long ZPZ-to-Engine campaign rather than a court or Gondiar-politics lead; public summaries keep her tied to the Hoa Quinzu / ghost-and-sabersone operational beat.
