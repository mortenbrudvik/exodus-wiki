Inessa (mindline originator name)

Inessa-Pierina is the Now and Forever Queen of Cheluli, one of the Crown Dominion’s peer queens. She appears in public character lists as a full royal seat; the novel stages fewer on-page scenes at her court than at Wynid, Verak, or Nizinsk.

Peer queen of the Accord

The Dominion’s balance depends on multiple Now and Forever Queens rotating the empress role and massing fleets at Kelowan. Soft uncertainty applies to Inessa’s private policies; confirmed is her status as major mindline queen of Cheluli.
