Acelynn

Acelynn

Role
Chief Archon of Queen Carolien-Amaia of Verak
Affiliation
Verak court; Imperial Palace politics at Kelowan
Mindline status
Major mindline

Acelynn is Chief Archon to Now and Forever Queen Carolien-Amaia of Verak — the Verak counterpart to Helena’s Gahiji. Acelynn is one of the major Crown mindlines of the Archimedes Engine era.

Verak’s chief operator

While detailed archon scenes often follow Wynid and foreign peers, Acelynn is the named institutional mind beside the rotating Empress. Carolien’s Gondiar crackdown, Kelowan fleet exercises, and collapse under kestrel-sprite sabotage all pass through Verak/imperial staff structures Acelynn represents.
