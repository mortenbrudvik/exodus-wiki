Carolien-Amaia of Verak holds the rotating imperial throne of the Crown Dominion for a sixty-year term during the novel’s main timeline. Palace politics at Kelowan, helium-economy stakes, and fleet exercises define her reign; under hardline pressure she authorizes a crackdown on Gondiar. During the Dolod / Boksrock endgame her position collapses amid palace sabotage and system infection, while Helena-Thyra celebrates hardline gains.
