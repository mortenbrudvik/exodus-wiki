Siskala

Siskala

Role
Major of the Wynid Royal Tiger Guard
Relation
Daughter of Helena-Chione; “new life” (not mindline host)
Affiliation
Wynid court military

Siskala is Major of the Wynid Royal Tiger Guard and a daughter of Queen Helena-Chione classified as new life — not selected as mindline host. She is the court’s professional military face rather than a congregant-trial rival for the crown.

Tiger Guard and new life

Imperial Celestial culture routes some offspring into mindline candidacy and others into “new life” careers. Siskala’s Tiger Guard majorate places her as a key military figure around Helena’s household and honor formations. She speaks less on the page than Thyra or Clavissa, and her importance is the institutional armed continuity of Wynid queenship.
