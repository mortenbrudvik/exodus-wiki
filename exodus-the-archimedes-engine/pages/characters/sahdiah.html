Sahdiah

Sahdiah

Role
Archon of the Talloch-Te (nomad trader dominion)
Affiliation
Celestials (Talloch-Te)
Crown alignment
Verak / Carolien-Amaia orbit (helium & shipbuilding)
Assets
Marcellu; Medusa; tasking via Andino
Agenda
Suppress ZPZ “random factors”; investigate Marcellu’s murder; Dolod endgame

Sahdiah is the Talloch-Te archon who uses Traveler logistics as deniable sensors — ordering interference against the Diligent’s ZPZ bid, tasking Medusa after Marcellu’s death, and burning for Dolod when Terence’s warning reaches him.

Talloch-Te and Crown politics

Excerpt diplomacy frames Talloch-Te as a major ship-fabrication dominion and heavy helium-3 consumer. Verak aligns with Talloch-Te; Wynid with Heresy. Sahdiah is the Talloch-Te intelligence face of that rivalry.

ZPZ denial and Marcellu

Sahdiah asks Marcellu (with Andino on Arcadia’s Moon) to prevent the Diligent from acquiring a ZPZ generator. After Liliana kills Marcellu with a cherenkov blade, Sahdiah summons Medusa, suspects Olomo, and sets her to investigate.

Endgame convergence

Medusa’s late message sends Sahdiah toward Dolod. He and Olomo trade accusations before realizing neither authored the Engine crisis. Asahi-Iryna is later dispatched after both foreign archons and the fleeing arkship.
