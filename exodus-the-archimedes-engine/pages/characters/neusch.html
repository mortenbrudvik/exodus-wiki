Neusch

Neusch

Relation
Son of Makaio-Faraji; recipient of neural gifts
Role
Continues Makaio protocols after assassination
Affiliation
Wynid archon household; residual opposition
Endgame
Uixic investigation; provides Terence the Aecus; aids Clavissa

Neusch is Makaio-Faraji’s son who already received neural gifts before the archon’s death. When the Makaio-Spirit activates inside Terence, Neusch becomes the living continuation of Makaio’s work.

Heir of a spymaster line

Neusch provides Terence the Aecus, shares information with Clavissa, and carries residual requests to dig into Bekket-network crimes. He is Celestial youth pressed into adult Great Game residue.
