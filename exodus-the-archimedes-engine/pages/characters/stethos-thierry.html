Stethos (minor)

Lord Stethos-Thierry is court datamaster to Queen Helena-Chione: a permanently interfaced information officer who answers her factual queries so she need not touch networks herself.

Datamaster of Wynid

Stethos stands shorter than his ~3 m queen, in scarlet-and-grey robes with bloodstone petals and a permanent connection bulb. He identifies Luus-Kinza’s palace expansions, offers archive searches, and fields reports including Makaio’s return and the Olomo meeting.
