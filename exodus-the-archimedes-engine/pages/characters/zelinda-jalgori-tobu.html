Zelinda Jalgori-Tobu

Role
Heir of the Jalgori-Tobu household
Affiliation
Uranics; client aristocracy on Gondiar
Family
Sibling of Finn and Otylia; Marchioness’s line
Home
Zetian Palace / Santa Rosa region, Gondiar

Zelinda Jalgori-Tobu is the designated heir of the Jalgori-Tobu client aristocracy on Gondiar — the institutional face of Uranic rule while twins Finn and Otylia take more mobile or ministerial paths. She appears in detective and court channels as a constrained official contact: early in the plot she tells Terence Wilson-Fletcher that the Governor’s office has ordered the offworld figure Dagon not pursued.
