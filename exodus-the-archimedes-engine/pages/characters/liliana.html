Liliana

Partners

Liliana is a mercenary whose trail runs through the novel’s heist, salvage, and crackdown threads. Early on she dumps Finn from a plane over Anoosha after a failed job; later she kills Marcellu with a cherenkov blade, surfaces in Terence’s facial-match investigations (linked to old Gyvoy meetings), and — with Toše — “protects” and extracts Josias Aponi during the Gondiar strike crisis aboard Andino’s disguised Arcadia’s Moon.
