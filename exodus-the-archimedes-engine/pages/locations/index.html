Locations

Systems, worlds, and ships of the far-future Centauri Cluster — from Crown Dominion capitals and agricultural worlds to the gas giant Dolod, the arkship Diligent, and the Traveler and archon hulls that carry the plot between them.

Worlds & systems

  • Centauri Cluster — the dense star field that is the whole stage
  • Crown Dominion systems — the multi-star territory the Imperial Accord shares in rotation
  • Kelowan — the capital system; where HeSea wealth is administered and the fleets mass
  • Wynid — Helena-Chione’s royal seat; the trials and the coup
  • Gondiar — the human client world: Santa Rosa, the Jalgori-Tobus, the lockdown
  • Hafnir — Finn’s domain, offered to the Diligent’s settlers
  • Anoosha — where the Camurdy Mountains throw Finn together with the arkship crew
  • Dolod — the iron exotic gas giant steered by an ancient Archimedes Engine
  • Boksrock — the body that Engine is finally turned against

Ships & vessels

  • Arkship Diligent — the generation ship at the centre of the plot
  • Arcadia’s Moon — Andino’s Traveler hull; archon errands and fugitives
  • Lestari — the Enfoe dynasty ship that carries the ZPZ salvage run
  • Alumata — Makaio-Faraji’s ship, where an archon briefs his instrument
  • Polkadav — gets the rest of the Jalgori-Tobus off Gondiar
  • Cybele’s Eagle — a deniable hull out of the lockdown, notable for its passengers

Other named hulls appear in passing: the Gansvoort (its captain is Toše’s contact), the Xirya (Olomo’s vantage during the Kelowan exercises), the Aecus (lent to Terence by Neusch), the dreadnaught Dracaenae, the Green Bounty, and the Enfoe-chartered Ilum. The Arcadia’s Moon also flies as the Infinite Totality.
