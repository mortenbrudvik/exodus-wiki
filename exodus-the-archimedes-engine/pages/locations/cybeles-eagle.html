Cybele’s Eagle

Cybele’s Eagle

Type
Deniable hull; passenger transport off Gondiar
Context
General Avone-Valerio’s navy lockdown
Known passenger
Liliana, under disguise
Investigated by
Terence Wilson-Fletcher
Significance
Half of the facial match that closes the detective thread

The Cybele’s Eagle is one of the deniable hulls moving people off Gondiar during the navy lockdown. It matters less for what it is than for who is aboard: Liliana, travelling under disguise, whose face on that departure becomes the evidence that ties a decades-long mercenary trail together.

Escape under lockdown

When General Avone-Valerio’s navy seals Gondiar, movement offworld runs through hulls willing to carry passengers who should not be leaving. Liliana boards the Cybele’s Eagle under disguise in the same operation in which Toše slips Terence’s surveillance and plants the orbital-capsule bomb that kills Lucia and hundreds of others.

The ship is a working part of the Gyvoy Enfoe network’s logistics rather than a Traveler charter with a named captain — the kind of hull the Traveler economy supplies to anyone who needs a departure not to be examined too closely.

The facial match

Terence’s late investigation pairs two images: Liliana at the old Gyvoy financial symposium years earlier, and the Cybele’s Eagle escapee. The match is one of the detective thread’s key closures — proof that the same operator who dumped Finn over Anoosha in the novel’s opening is still steering events as Gondiar burns.

Through Medusa’s admissions the same manhunt ties the bombings, the Cybele’s Eagle, and Makaio-Faraji’s sniper death into a single network rather than three unrelated crimes.
