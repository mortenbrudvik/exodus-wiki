Anoosha

Anoosha

Anoosha is the world where the novel’s human threads first collide: Finn Jalgori-Tobu is dumped injured over the Camurdy Mountains after a failed heist, and crash-landed crew from the arkship Diligent — including Eleanor Aponi and Josias Aponi — find him. The rescue seeds the Hafnir land deal and the long alliance that drives the Engine plot.

The world itself

Anoosha is a client world of about 1.15 g, heavy enough that its gravity is a working condition rather than a detail. Its wealth is ore: mining and refining licensed to the Verak Great Families, with Uranic commercial houses shipping the output to Kelowan industry. That monopoly is what makes Dolod a political problem as well as an orbital one — an iron exotic settling into the capital system threatens to cheapen exactly the ore Verak controls, which is why the Crown Dominion’s helium and ore politics run through this planet at all.

Above the surface, the orbital city of Pana-Seak is where Traveler and archon business touches Anoosha directly: Marcellu is murdered there, and the investigation into his death pulls Medusa and the Talloch-Te archon network into the novel’s middle act.

Camurdy Mountains and the dump

The novel opens with Finn bound and thrown from a plane over the Camurdy range after a failed operation involving the mercenary Liliana. Alone and injured in wilderness, he demonstrates Uranic competence with Celestial materials by shaping livestone into shelter — an early showcase of neural interface skill that later marks him as useful to both human settlers and manipulators.

Anoosha itself sits under Crown Dominion–era human settlement patterns: not a royal capital like Kelowan or Wynid, but a working world where Uranic adventure, mercenary plots, and later-arriving arkship landings can intersect without immediate imperial ceremony.

Diligent crash and alliance

Ellie and Josias find Finn after their spaceplane crash on Anoosha. The meeting is practical before it is political: survival, medical care, and a bargain. Josias wants land and a future for Diligent colonists; Finn wants a path off-world and leverage against the constraints of client-rule life on Gondiar. The deal they frame — settlement at Hafnir in exchange for Finn’s stakes and mobility — becomes the novel’s central human compact.

From Anoosha the plot leaves for Gondiar’s courts and estates, Traveler salvage yards, and eventually Dolod, but the Camurdy rescue remains the origin of trust between Jalgori-Tobu and Aponi lines. Without Anoosha’s crash geography, the Diligent never becomes Finn’s ship of fortune, and the later Engine conspiracy lacks its human core.
