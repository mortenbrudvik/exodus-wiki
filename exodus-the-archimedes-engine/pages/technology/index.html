Technology

Landmark devices and systems of the setting: world-moving engines, Celestial succession tech, self-shaping materials, power plants, drives, and neural interfaces that shape both daily life and the novel’s endgame.

Megastructures

  • Archimedes Engine — Elohim-built system for moving planetary bodies; the lever Finn pulls at Dolod

Ships and power

  • ZPZ generator — the plant the Diligent must have to become a true deep-space vessel
  • Entropy drive — the propulsion class traded away to obtain it

Minds and materials

  • Mindline — the succession system that makes Celestial queenship both throne and attack surface
  • Neural interface — the brain–machine layer of Cluster life, and the means of coercion at the novel’s centre
  • Livestone — self-shaping silicate that turns architecture into an act of will
