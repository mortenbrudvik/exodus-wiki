Technology

Landmark devices and systems of the setting: world-moving engines, Celestial succession tech, self-shaping materials, power plants, drives, and neural interfaces that shape both daily life and the novel’s endgame.

Megastructures

  • Archimedes Engine

    Elohim-built system for moving planetary bodies; the lever Finn pulls at Dolod

Ships and power

  • ZPZ generator

    The plant the Diligent must have to become a true deep-space vessel

  • Entropy drive

    The propulsion class traded away to obtain it

Minds and materials

  • Mindline

    The succession system that makes Celestial queenship both throne and attack surface

  • Neural interface

    The brain–machine layer of Cluster life, and the means of coercion at the novel’s centre

  • Livestone

    Self-shaping silicate that turns architecture into an act of will

Other technology appears in passing: kestrel sprites (atom-thin surveillance clouds made on Uixic, released above the Imperial Palace as the sabotage that infects Carolien-Amaia’s evacuation ship), anchor ships (the hulls that cage Engine geometry over Boksrock), rekaul (the neural drug drawn from the Moaksha Changelings), and induction pads (the point of contact of the neural interface, forced on Finn by Gyvoy Enfoe).
