Ordered markers for the history behind and within Exodus: The Archimedes Engine. Macro context (Sol exodus, Centauri Cluster settlement, Celestial dominions) is brief; most rows cover the novel’s multi-decade span from Dolod detection through the Archimedes Engine climax. All calendar years are approximate: they are reconstructed from “N years ago” dialogue spoken under heavy time dilation, so the figures below are working labels rather than a canon calendar.

Macro context (pre-novel)

Period / label Event Links
~40,000 years before main action Humanity flees a dying Earth on arkships; early fleets settle the dense Centauri Cluster. First-wave descendants evolve into Celestials and found dominions under systems such as the Imperial Accord. Centauri Cluster; Celestials; Crown Dominion
Long settlement era Later arkship waves arrive as Uranic and baseline humans under Celestial client rule. Megastructures (including Archimedes Engines) reshape orbits and economies across the Cluster. Uranics; Archimedes Engine; Crown Dominion systems

Notes on chronology

  • Relativistic travel means shipboard months can equal years of Cluster history — Finn’s ZPZ and Kingsnest arcs leave Gondiar politics transformed on return.
  • Court, detective, and arkship threads are concurrent; the table is a reading-order scaffold, not a claim of perfect simultaneity.
