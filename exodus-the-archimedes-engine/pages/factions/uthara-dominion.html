Uthara Dominion

Uthara Dominion

Type
Celestial dominion (annexation target in hardline plans)
Book role
Named with Capo Frois as region Thyra proposes to annex
Strategic frame
Mara Yama’s “original target” before Hoa Quinzu pause (recap)
Soft uncertainty
Little on-page internal culture; known via Crown expansion rhetoric

The Uthara Dominion is a neighboring Celestial polity that appears in Helena-Thyra’s expansion program: annex Capo Frois and Uthara to grow economy and workforce ahead of a future Mara Yama war — including false-flag talk that would destabilize the region on purpose.

Pawn on the hardline board

Uthara is not given a full court cast in public recaps. Its importance is geopolitical: hardliners treat it as annexable mass, and timeline material links Mara Yama’s earlier vector toward Uthara before activity concentrates near Hoa Quinzu. The Diligent’s late flight toward Capo Frois places human rebels on the edge of the same contested map.
