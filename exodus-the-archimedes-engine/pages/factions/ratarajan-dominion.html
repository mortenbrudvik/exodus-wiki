Ratarajan Dominion

Ratarajan Dominion

Type
Celestial dominion
Book theatre
Hoa Quinzu system — warns Diligent of Mara Yama presence
Role
Local authority / intelligence touch during Kajval campaign

The Ratarajan Dominion is the Celestial power that warns the Diligent in the Hoa Quinzu system that Mara Yama forces are present — orbiting de Verya to refuel — just as Finn and Ellie’s team stage the Kajval Celestial dropship theft.

Hoa Quinzu contact

The Ratarajan appearance is brief but structural: it proves Mara Yama activity is not only Wynid propaganda, and it raises the stakes of the Kajval extraction under ghost and saberstone fire. Ratarajan internal politics stay offstage; their role is that of the local dominion voice the Diligent must heed while deep in foreign space.
