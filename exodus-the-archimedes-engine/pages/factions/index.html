Factions

Powers and peoples of the Centauri Cluster in Exodus: The Archimedes Engine — post-human Celestial dominions, the Crown alliance they form, Uranic client societies, free-ranging Travelers, and the overlapping currents of human liberation. Full spoilers throughout.

Ruling order

  • Celestials — Transhuman clades (Crown, Heresy, Talloch-Te, Remnant past); mindlines, archons, Great Game
  • Crown Dominion — Imperial Accord of queens; Kelowan capital; HeSea helium monopoly; human client worlds

Human layers

  • Uranics — Intermediate humans with neural interfaces; proxy aristocrats on Gondiar and Anoosha
  • Travelers — Ship owners; Remnant hunters; ZPZ free agents between Dominions
  • Human liberation — Regal Democrats, underground nets, strikes, Engine plots; Celestial countermeasures
