Travelers are interstellar free agents and ship-centred cultures that move between Dominions as explorers, freighters, and deniable operators in Exodus: The Archimedes Engine. They are neither Crown subjects nor a single polity: ships such as Andino’s Arcadia’s Moon bargain tech, ferry fugitives, and intervene in salvage races where ZPZ power and Engine logistics can tip the Great Game.

Free agents between Dominions

In a Cluster ruled by Celestial houses, Travelers occupy the seams: trade routes, salvage yards, mercenary contracts, and off-book errands for archons who need distance from formal fleets. They can aid human actors one season and hunt the same hulls the next — loyalty attaches to contract, reputation, and ship survival more than to constitutional causes.

That flexibility makes them useful and dangerous. Dominion hardliners treat some Traveler activity as a “random factor” to suppress; liberation networks use Traveler bioware, lifts, and weapons; investigators such as Terence Wilson-Fletcher keep finding Traveler-linked faces (mercenaries, ghosts, tasking from archon Sahdiah) inside Gondiar crimes.

Arcadia’s Moon and the ZPZ race

Captain Andino and the Arcadia’s Moon are the novel’s primary Traveler face. Early, Traveler interests (including Marcellu) try to stop the late arkship Diligent from acquiring a ZPZ generator — advanced drive/power tech that would let a baseline-origin hull punch above its expected weight. At Terrik Papuan the Arcadia’s Moon tails the Lestari carrying Finn and Ellie; fire is exchanged; ghost fleets assault the salvage.

Later Andino is hired again to track and disable the Diligent’s ZPZ. Associates die (Marcellu to Liliana’s cherenkov blade); Medusa is taken aboard after Sahdiah’s summons. The ship also runs under disguise (e.g. Infinite Totality) when ferrying Josias Aponi and mercenaries off Gondiar after the strike broadcast — Traveler logistics as escape hatch for political explosives.

Endgame logistics and ambiguous motives

As the Dolod / Archimedes Engine crisis peaks, Traveler hulls remain in the logistics web: tracking Engine OS paths, carrying dangerous passengers, and positioning for post-climax demonstrations (Andino aiming antimatter power near Capo Frois in closing beats). Whether Travelers serve a coherent pan-Cluster interest, archon clients, or pure self-preservation is left deliberately ambiguous in public summaries.

For wiki purposes, treat Travelers as a faction of method rather than ideology: high mobility, advanced ship tech, and willingness to sell services to every side of Crown Dominion and liberation politics. Links to specific captains and contracts matter more than a single Traveler manifesto.
