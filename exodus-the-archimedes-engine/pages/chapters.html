Full-spoiler, reading-order summaries for Exodus: The Archimedes Engine. The novel is long and interleaves many short POV chapters across concurrent threads (notably Finn / Diligent, Terence, and Thyra’s court). The tables below are organised as sequential summary units in story order rather than as numbered chapters.

Spoilers: This page covers the entire novel, including the ending.

Opening and early setup

# Focus Summary Links
1 Frame / Cluster The opening establishes the far-future Centauri Cluster, Celestial rule, and the scale of human settlement after the Sol exodus, setting the board for Crown Dominion politics and late arkships. Centauri Cluster; Celestials
2 Court / Dolod seed Celestial viewpoint material introduces long-term strategic interest in Dolod and Archimedes Engines — megastructures that can alter trajectories and underwrite dominion economies. Dolod; Archimedes Engine; Kelowan
3 Finn in crisis Finn Jalgori-Tobu is dumped in the wilderness on Anoosha after a failed heist involving mercenary Liliana. Injured and desperate, he uses neural tools and livestone to survive until rescue. Finn; Anoosha; Liliana; Livestone
4 Diligent arrives The late arkship Diligent reaches the Kelowan system (Kinnox orbit). Josias and Ellie Aponi crash a spaceplane on Anoosha and find Finn. Diligent; Josias; Ellie
5 Hafnir deal Finn bargains: family land at Hafnir for the Diligent and a settlement future for its people. Ambition, land politics, and mutual need bind the parties. Hafnir; Gondiar
6 Coronation era Imperial succession theater: Carolien-Amaia takes the rotating Empress role for the next sixty-year cycle. Court alliances, including Helena-Chione’s personal and political orbit, shift. Carolien-Amaia; Helena-Chione; Crown Dominion
7 Thyra’s birth / childhood Thyra is spawned into Helena-Chione’s congregant line. Early cunning and rivalry with siblings (including Clavissa) foreshadow her path through the princess trials. Thyra; Clavissa; Bekket

Gondiar, networks, and reclaiming the ship

# Focus Summary Links
8 Terence’s beat Detective Terence Wilson-Fletcher works Santa Rosa’s underworld, moving through shootouts, informants, and dead ends under pressure from powers that do not want certain offworld contacts pursued. Terence; Gondiar
9 Return to Gondiar Finn, Josias, and Ellie reach Gondiar. Josias sells the Hafnir/Diligent arrangement to the Marchioness as stability and human-economy opportunity. Escort and court optics matter. Zelinda; Otylia
10 Reclaiming Diligent Chartered shipping (Enfoe connections) and Imperial escort bring the arkship under control. Crew futures split between settling Hafnir and staying shipboard. Dejean; Diligent
11 Alliances form Refit politics, entropy-drive bargains, and a path toward a ZPZ generator emerge. Terence is drawn deeper into Celestial loyalty tests and intelligence work. Entropy drive; ZPZ generator

Trials, salvage, and parallel conspiracies

# Focus Summary Links
12 First princess trial Combat trial on Wynid. Thyra uses neural dominance of mounts to slaughter congregant sisters and advance her claim; court horror and suspicion begin to accumulate. Thyra; Wynid; Neural interface
13 Traveler watchers Outside the Crown’s official story, Traveler and archon networks (including Andino / Arcadia’s Moon and archon Sahdiah’s middleman Marcellu, who hires that hull) try to steer whether the Diligent acquires critical tech. Andino; Travelers; Olomo
14 Terrik Papuan salvage Finn and Ellie ride the Enfoe hull Lestari to the ZPZ salvage and meet ambushes, ghosts, and double-crosses. Casualties and stolen relics raise the cost of the Diligent’s freedom. ZPZ; Ellie; Lestari
15 Hafnir years Settlement grows; Josias and Otylia marry; human political parties and liberation sentiment thicken. Terence’s family life and career advance while surveillance of mercenaries continues. Hafnir; Human liberation
16 Second trial Survival trial kills more congregants. Thyra masks her hand, spares Clavissa for optics, and deepens court feuds. Bekket’s secrets about Thyra’s heritage surface. Clavissa; Bekket
17 Return with ZPZ After relativistic lag, Finn and Ellie bring a ZPZ home. Drive trades complete; Gyvoy pushes a further quest for Archimedes Engine OS materials at Kingsnest. Diligent; Archimedes Engine
18 Liliana / Marcellu Covert violence spreads among the fixers. Liliana kills Marcellu, and Medusa and Sahdiah’s network continue the chase for culprits and leverage. Liliana; Medusa

Mid-book expansion: Engine OS, trials, and hardline rise

# Focus Summary Links
19 Hoa Quinzu extraction The dropship heist on Kajval involves ghosts, saberstones, and Elsbeth McQuillan’s armored rescue. The team steals a Celestial dropship under extreme fire. Elsbeth; Finn
20 Third trial / princess Political trial at another queen’s court. Thyra outmaneuvers rivals with gossip and analysis, is elevated toward princess status, and receives deeper mindline gifts. Thyra; Mindline
21 Uulana investigation Uulana-Lyon’s inquiry into Bekket and Thyra ends in assassination and ship destruction — proof that court opposition is being erased. Wynid; Uulana; Bekket; Thyra
22 Steam-room coup Thyra ambushes Helena-Chione, hijacks the mindline, and becomes Helena-Thyra, Queen of Wynid. Bekket rises; hardliners reshape the court. Helena-Chione; Thyra; Mindline
23 Kingsnest factory The Diligent quest reaches Kingsnest’s Archimedes Engine factory complex. Finn interfaces for OS data amid pirates, ghosts, and nightweid horrors; the crew pays a bloody price. Archimedes Engine; Neural interface
24 Tinaja / HeSea intel Archon science threads follow Dolod’s path, helium-sea anomalies, unknown ships, and Heresy/Crown information warfare, with Olomo and allies gathering strategic edges. Olomo; Dolod
25 Thyra consolidates Years of hardline rule bring chief archon replacements shot through with identity theft and neural takeover, leave Clavissa a constrained survivor at court, and aim militarized ambition at neighbors. Lord Gahiji; Clavissa

Late book: crackdown, Dolod climax, aftermath

# Focus Summary Links
26 Gondiar investigations deepen Bombings, false identities (Gyvoy imposture), Medusa custody games, and archon task-force politics push Terence into existential choices. Terence; Medusa
27 Makaio assassination Makaio-Faraji is sniped; a dying neural transfer leaves a “Makaio-Spirit” aspect in Terence, fusing detective and Celestial counter-intelligence agendas. Terence; Neural interface
28 Navy lockdown Imperial hardliners lock Gondiar down. Jalgori-Tobu displacement, ghost patrols, and constitutional strike rhetoric collide. The Marchioness and others die in escalating airstrikes and raids. Gondiar; Human liberation
29 Escapes Several flight paths run at once: Andino’s ship travels disguised, the Polkadav waits for the Diligent, and Terence makes covert moves with Medusa and Neusch. Fleets gather toward Kelowan for annexation exercises. Andino; Otylia
30 Diligent returns After roughly thirty-two years of world-time out-system, the Diligent re-enters the crisis. Family reunions collide with atrocity broadcasts and the final Engine plan. Diligent; Finn
31 Dolod boarding Finn, Gyvoy, and allies board Archimedes Engine infrastructure on Dolod. Gyvoy stokes vengeance; Finn interfaces with the Engine and issues commands that do not match the simple “stop Dolod” brief. Dolod; Archimedes Engine
32 Betrayal & rekaul Gyvoy abandons Finn after the interface. Ellie recovers suppressed memories of Gyvoy forcing Finn’s induction pad and wiping her mind — realizing Gyvoy is a Celestial operator. Ellie; Neural interface
33 Anchor ships / Boksrock Engine commands redirect momentum transfers onto Boksrock, putting it on a path toward Kelowan while anchor ships cage the geometry. The Diligent uses ZPZ and Engine-linked acceleration to escape capture. Kelowan; Boksrock; ZPZ
34 Imperial crisis Empress Carolien’s position collapses amid sabotage (kestrel sprites) and cascading fleet politics. Thyra’s gambit and human-adjacent operations leave the Dominion in chaos. Carolien-Amaia; Thyra
35 Open ending The Diligent and allies vector toward Capo Frois and further conflict. Resistance is not finished; the board is set for wider war and the sequel’s stakes. Plot summary; Timeline
