Full-spoiler, reading-order summaries for Exodus: The Archimedes Engine. The novel is long and interleaves many short POV chapters across concurrent threads (notably Finn / Diligent, Terence, and Thyra’s court). The tables below are organized as sequential summary units in story order rather than as numbered chapters, so the numbers are this wiki’s own and will not match your copy.

Spoilers: This page covers the entire novel, including the ending.

Opening: the Cluster, the fall, and the bargain

# Focus Summary Links
1 Frame / Cluster The novel opens on the Centauri Cluster tens of thousands of years after humanity fled Earth. First-wave descendants have evolved into Celestials who rule dominions of client humans, while later arkships are still arriving to find no empty worlds waiting for them. Centauri Cluster; Celestials; Uranics; Crown Dominion
2 Court / Dolod seed Decades before the main action, Heresy archon Olomo meets Wynid’s Makaio-Yalbo at a remote Tinaja Lagrangian point and reveals that the iron exotic gas giant Dolod, still carrying an Elohim Archimedes Engine, is on course into the Kelowan system. That intelligence starts the novel’s strategic clock. Olomo; Makaio; Dolod; Archimedes Engine; Kelowan; Heresy Dominion
3 Finn in crisis Finn Jalgori-Tobu, Minsterialis of Hafnir on Gondiar, is bound and thrown from a plane over Anoosha’s Camurdy Mountains after a heist with the mercenary Liliana goes wrong. Injured and hunted by exposure, he shapes livestone into shelter and works his neural interface to stay alive. Finn; Anoosha; Liliana; Livestone; Neural interface; Gondiar
4 Diligent arrives The late arkship Diligent reaches the Kelowan system and parks at Kinnox. Josias Aponi and his granddaughter Ellie crash a spaceplane on Anoosha, find Finn in the wilderness, and treat him with a casual generosity that marks the gap between arkship humans and Uranic clients. Diligent; Josias; Ellie; Kelowan; Anoosha; Uranics
5 Hafnir deal Finn and Josias strike the bargain the rest of the book turns on: Jalgori-Tobu land at Hafnir for the Diligent’s colonists, in exchange for Finn’s path off-world and leverage against the proxy role his family plays for the Crown. Hafnir; Finn; Josias; Diligent; Uranics
6 Coronation era Imperial succession at Kelowan: Carolien-Amaia of Verak receives the rotating Empress throne from Luus-Kinza of Bassa for a sixty-year term. Around the same court cycle, Helena-Chione of Wynid takes Lord Bekket of Uixic as a coronation-night partner. Carolien-Amaia; Luus; Helena-Chione; Bekket; Crown Dominion; Kelowan
7 Thyra’s birth and childhood Thyra is born into Helena-Chione’s congregant line the following year — Bekket’s daughter, though the court does not know it. Childhood rivalry among the sisters, Clavissa among them, sets up the trials that will decide which congregants rise. Thyra; Clavissa; Bekket; Helena-Chione; Wynid

Gondiar, networks, and reclaiming the ship

# Focus Summary Links
8 Terence’s beat Detective Terence Wilson-Fletcher works Santa Rosa’s underworld on Gondiar. After he arrests the gang figure Younes, Lieutenant Colvin orders Younes to introduce the offworld contact Dagon to Gyvoy Enfoe; the surveillance fails, Younes is murdered, and Colvin self-immolates in custody. Governor-level orders shut the trail down. Terence; Gondiar; Dagon; Gyvoy Enfoe
9 Return to Gondiar Finn, Josias and Ellie reach Gondiar, where Josias sells the Hafnir arrangement to the Marchioness Mary Kathleen Jalgori-Tobu as stability and human-economy opportunity. Finn’s heir sister Zelinda and his twin Otylia weigh what the deal costs the family’s standing. Gondiar; Zelinda; Otylia; Josias; Hafnir
10 Reclaiming Diligent Chartered Enfoe shipping and an Imperial navy escort bring the arkship back under its owners’ control, the escort disabling rival entropy drives on the way. Dejean keeps the ship running as its crew splits between settling Hafnir and staying aboard. Diligent; Dejean; Gyvoy Enfoe; Hafnir; Entropy drive
11 Alliances form The Gath refit the arkship. Gyvoy Enfoe brokers the trade the plot needs — a path to a Terrik Papuan ZPZ generator in exchange for the Diligent’s entropy drives — while Terence is drawn deeper into Celestial loyalty tests and archon intelligence work. Gyvoy Enfoe; ZPZ generator; Entropy drive; Diligent; Terence

Trials, salvage, and parallel conspiracies

# Focus Summary Links
12 First princess trial The combat trial on Wynid. Thyra takes neural control of the mounts and turns them on her competing sisters, advancing her claim as the court’s suspicion begins to gather. Terence’s Wynid loyalty test overlaps the same trial, and it is there he first meets archon Makaio-Faraji, who will later summon him to meetings aboard the Alumata. Thyra; Wynid; Neural interface; Terence; Makaio; Alumata
13 Traveler watchers Outside the Crown’s official story, Traveler and archon networks work to keep a ZPZ out of human hands. Talloch-Te archon Sahdiah treats the Diligent as a “random factor” to be removed from the Great Game and tasks his middleman Marcellu, who hires captain Andino and the Arcadia’s Moon. Sahdiah; Marcellu; Andino; Arcadia’s Moon; Travelers; Talloch-Te Dominion; ZPZ generator
14 Terrik Papuan salvage Finn, Ellie and Toše ride the Enfoe hull Lestari to the salvage. Andino and Marcellu’s crews attack, ghost fleets assault the wreck, and fire is exchanged across a job that stretches into a multi-year expedition under time dilation. Finn; Ellie; Toše; Lestari; Andino; Marcellu; ZPZ generator
15 Hafnir years While the salvage runs, the settlement grows. Josias marries Otylia, builds the Regal Democrats into a party, and wins a seat on the Governor’s Human Affairs Advisory Council. Terence marries the forensic tech Jimena and follows liberation funding trails that point offworld to Tinaja, while QIX — Queen In Exile sentiment — rises in the underworld alongside the legal parties. Hafnir; Josias; Otylia; Terence; Human liberation
16 Second trial The survival trial on Binji. Thyra arranges killings through non-awakened bloodlines while sparing Clavissa for the political credit of having “saved” a sister. Bekket’s revelations about her heritage reframe what she is really competing for. Thyra; Clavissa; Bekket; Wynid
17 Return with ZPZ Finn and Ellie bring the generator home after years of world-time and months of ship-time. The drive trade completes, and Gyvoy immediately pushes the next objective: Archimedes Engine operating software from the factory complex at Kingsnest. Finn; Ellie; Gyvoy Enfoe; ZPZ generator; Archimedes Engine; Diligent
18 Liliana and Marcellu Violence spreads through the fixers. Liliana kills Marcellu; Sahdiah tasks Marcellu’s former right hand Medusa with finding the killer, and the hunt for culprits and leverage runs on under Traveler cover. Liliana; Marcellu; Medusa; Sahdiah

Engine software, the coup, and hardline rule

# Focus Summary Links
19 Kajval extraction In the Hoa Quinzu system, Finn’s team steals a Celestial dropship on Kajval under heavy fire, through ghost units and saberstone weapons, with Elsbeth McQuillan running the drive team and making the armoured rescue that gets them out. Finn; Elsbeth McQuillan
20 Third trial and elevation The politics trial at Queen Ramona-Ursule’s court on Nizinsk. Thyra outmanoeuvres multi-house rivals with gossip and analysis, wins elevation to princess, and receives a deep mindline gift from Helena — the channel she will later turn on its owner. Thyra; Ramona-Ursule; Helena-Chione; Mindline
21 Uulana investigation Uulana-Lyon, the court parent whose daughter Delfina dies in the Binji trial, investigates Bekket and Thyra. Bekket’s Guillrameo network answers with a slowball ambush: Uulana-Lyon is assassinated on a Uixic inquiry and his ship is wrecked and discarded at Tonobo. Uulana; Bekket; Thyra; Wynid
22 Steam-room coup Thyra ambushes Helena-Chione in a steam room, hijacks the mindline, and rules Wynid as Helena-Thyra — a composite in which how much of Helena survives is never resolved. Bekket rises to master of the court and hardliners reshape Wynid politics. Thyra; Helena-Chione; Bekket; Mindline; Wynid
23 Kingsnest factory The Diligent’s quest reaches the Archimedes Engine factory complex at Kingsnest. Finn works his neural interface to pull the operating software while pirates, ghosts and nightweid — swarming winged predators — make the derelict lethal, and the crew pays for the data in casualties. Finn; Diligent; Archimedes Engine; Neural interface
24 Tinaja and HeSea intelligence Heresy research teams under Olomo work the Pillar of Zeus and the HeSea, confirming Dolod’s track, its iron-exotic composition, and the Engine burn that turned it toward Kelowan seven thousand years earlier. Handing that to Wynid forces a Crown crisis with no Heresy fingerprints on it. Olomo; Dolod; Archimedes Engine; Heresy Dominion; Crown Dominion; Kelowan
25 Thyra consolidates Years of hardline rule. Chief Archon Gahiji is displaced by Uulana-Shoigu — an infiltrated identity with Iuntin-Detlef behind the mask — annexation and false-flag strategies are floated, and queen fleets are ordered to mass at Kelowan. Clavissa survives at court as a demoted nanny. Thyra; Lord Gahiji; Uulana; Clavissa; Kelowan

Crackdown, Dolod, and the open ending

# Focus Summary Links
26 Gondiar investigations deepen Terence’s later cases converge: the Bopbe kidnap, the arrest of Medusa, and bombings whose funding trails point offworld to Tinaja. He matches Liliana’s face to an old Gyvoy meeting, and works Medusa after she admits Sahdiah tasked her. Terence; Medusa; Liliana; Gyvoy Enfoe; Sahdiah
27 Makaio assassination Toše snipes Makaio-Faraji at the Governor’s mansion. A dying neural transfer leaves an aspect of him — the Makaio-Spirit — inside Terence, fusing a Gondiar detective with Celestial counter-intelligence and activating protocols with Makaio’s son Neusch. Makaio; Toše; Terence; Neusch; Neural interface
28 Navy lockdown Empress Carolien is pressured into a hard crackdown. General Avone-Valerio’s navy locks Gondiar down with ghost units and awakened animals, and the Jalgori-Tobus are displaced from Zetian Palace. Josias broadcasts a general strike as constitutional speech; retaliatory airstrikes kill Finn’s mother the Marchioness and Everett’s wife Variaka, and Toše’s orbital-capsule bomb kills hundreds, Terence’s right hand Lucia among them. Carolien-Amaia; Avone-Valerio; Josias; Toše; Gondiar; Human liberation
29 Escapes Several flight paths run at once: Josias goes out with Liliana and Toše aboard Andino’s disguised Arcadia’s Moon, other Jalgori-Tobus wait for the returning Diligent aboard the Polkadav, and Terence moves covertly with Medusa and Neusch. Liliana leaves under disguise aboard the Cybele’s Eagle, the hull that gives Terence his facial match. Thyra brings her fleet to Kelowan early, under cover of annexation exercises. Josias; Andino; Arcadia’s Moon; Polkadav; Terence; Liliana; Cybele’s Eagle; Thyra; Kelowan
30 Diligent returns The arkship comes back after roughly thirty-two years of world-time out-system, into a Dominion already in crisis. Finn’s reunion with what is left of his family collides with the atrocity broadcasts and with the Engine plan Gyvoy has spent years steering him toward. Diligent; Finn; Gyvoy Enfoe
31 Dolod boarding Finn, Gyvoy and allies board the Archimedes Engine infrastructure on Dolod. Gyvoy works on Finn’s grief over his mother and over Gondiar’s repression, and Finn issues commands that go well past the agreed plan of simply denying Dolod to Kelowan. Finn; Gyvoy Enfoe; Dolod; Archimedes Engine
32 Betrayal and recovered memory Gyvoy abandons Finn once the interface is done. Using rekaul, Ellie recovers memories she did not know were missing: Gyvoy forcing Finn’s induction pad, and wiping her own mind afterwards. Gyvoy is a Celestial operator, and the shell he wears answers to Dagon. Ellie; Finn; Gyvoy Enfoe; Dagon; Neural interface
33 Anchor ships and Boksrock Engine commands redirect momentum transfers onto Boksrock, putting it on a path into the capital system while anchor ships cage the geometry. Finn escapes by anchor ship, and a second transfer couples that anchor to the Diligent’s ZPZ field, accelerating the arkship at extreme g out of the trap. Boksrock; Kelowan; Finn; Diligent; ZPZ generator; Archimedes Engine
34 Imperial crisis Carolien’s position collapses: kestrel sprites sabotage the imperial palace, her evacuation fails under system infection, and the exposure of networks reaching as far as Dagon runs alongside Boksrock’s calculated trajectory. Thyra issues kill orders on the fleeing arkship and profits from every part of the outcome; whether she scripted the final geometry or merely exploited it is left open. Carolien-Amaia; Thyra; Dagon; Boksrock; Kelowan
35 Open ending The Diligent and the Arcadia’s Moon vector toward Capo Frois. Hardline Wynid power is stronger, the imperial centre is reeling, and Traveler and multi-archon games — Olomo, Sahdiah, Asahi-Iryna, and pursuit fleets — are all still running. The novel delivers no clean liberation victory. Diligent; Arcadia’s Moon; Olomo; Sahdiah; Asahi-Iryna; Human liberation
