Sources and provenance

Where this wiki’s content comes from, and which parts of it are reconstructions rather than statements you can rely on. Article pages elsewhere on this wiki state things plainly, without interrupting themselves to discuss their own sourcing; this page is where that information lives instead.

Primary source

The novel itself — Exodus: The Archimedes Engine by Peter F. Hamilton — is the only authority for anything in the story. Every character, place, faction and technology article is an original prose summary written for this wiki. Long quotations from the novel are deliberately avoided.

The book’s own “Timeline of the Centauri Cluster” is the authority for the macro history (Exodus era through the Present era). Where this wiki and the book disagree, the book is right. Corrections are expected.

Book, game, and franchise chronology

The novel and the forthcoming Archetype Entertainment game share a setting but are not the same story. The publisher and the game’s own site both call the novels prequels, which is a statement about release order rather than about in-story dates; readers and interviews have disagreed about where the books actually sit relative to the game’s present. This wiki does not invent a reconciliation, and no article here asserts an interval between the two.

  • In-story years and event order follow the novel (and this wiki’s reconstructed timeline labels — see below).
  • Franchise vocabulary from official game materials is used only where it matches or clearly extends terms the novel already establishes.
  • Real-world publication order and game release dates belong on Book, not in character or location articles.

Reconstructed, not canon

Two parts of this wiki are scaffolding built to make the story navigable. They are useful for finding your place; they are not the novel’s own structure, and they should not be quoted as fact.

Timeline years

The ~42,3xx figures are not dates the novel prints. They are reverse-engineered from in-story “N years ago” dialogue, spoken by characters whose own clocks disagree because of relativistic travel. The same approach appears in independent public recaps; agreement on a label does not make the number canon. Treat every year on that page as a working label for ordering events, not as a calendar. The ordering is more trustworthy than the numbers.

Chapter summaries

The numbered rows are sequential summary units invented for this wiki, not the novel’s chapters. The book interleaves many short point-of-view chapters across concurrent threads, and the units group them by thread and beat instead. Numbers will not line up with your copy.

What is not used

Unauthorized full-text copies of the novel (including dumps on code hosts) are not sources for this project. AI “book summary” sites and spoiler-free marketing blurbs alone are too thin or too error-prone to seed articles. Game trailers and creature shorts are flavour for the wider IP, not continuity for Hamilton’s plot.

What is not recorded

This wiki was seeded in July 2026, and the material consulted at the time was not logged line by line against the novel. The references above cover real-world publication facts and name the main secondary aids available for later verification. They do not mean every plot, character and setting claim on this site has been re-checked against the book since seeding.

So: where an article states a name, a relationship or a sequence of events, it is being reported in good faith but often without a citation behind it. The project README records the writing conventions, including the rule that a short accurate stub is preferred over an invented detail. Pages known to be thin say so with an explicit stub notice, as on Boksrock.
