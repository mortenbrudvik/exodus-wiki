Main Page

Spoilers: This wiki covers all of Exodus: The Archimedes Engine, including the ending. Articles contain full spoilers.

This is a personal full-spoiler reading companion for Peter F. Hamilton’s 2024 novel Exodus: The Archimedes Engine — a space-opera set in the far-future Centauri Cluster and a narrative prequel for the Exodus game universe. It covers people, places, factions, technology, timeline, and plot.

About this wiki

Articles are encyclopedic original summaries written for this wiki. Long novel quotations are avoided. Secondary topics may appear as short stubs until expanded. The Timeline years and the chapter divisions are reconstructed working labels rather than the novel’s own dates and table of contents — see Sources and provenance for what is verified and what is not. Header search works best when the site is opened through a local static server (see the project README).
